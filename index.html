RecallDeutsch

Random alarms during the day read out a few German verbs: the English meaning, a pause to answer aloud, then the German. Cards come from Verb Meister and Karteikasten and update by themselves.

Download the Android app

Android 8 or newer.

Install on the phone

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download the Android app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If the phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open RecallDeutsch → Settings → paste the Verb Meister sync key → Save and sync.
  4. Go through the Phone setup checklist: notifications, alarms & reminders, full-screen notifications, no battery restriction.
  5. On Oppo / OnePlus: in App info → Battery, allow background activity and auto-launch. Then lock RecallDeutsch in Recent apps.
  6. On the Today tab, switch Alarms on and try Recall now.

Updates: download again from this page and install over the old app. Your settings and progress stay.
